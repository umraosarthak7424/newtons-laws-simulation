First Law — Law of Inertia

An object stays at rest or continues at constant velocity unless an unbalanced external force acts on it.

Example: a book on a table remains at rest until someone moves it.

Quick Knowledge Check

Formula Revision

First law: If net force is zero, velocity remains constant.

Second law: F = ma (net force = mass × acceleration).

Third law: FAB = −FBA (equal and opposite forces on different objects).
